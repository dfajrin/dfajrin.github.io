About Me

Your consultant with international education and professional experience

Konsultan Pendidikan dan Karir Internasional

With a background in Meteorology from ITB Indonesia and a master's degree from Universität Hamburg, I bring both scientific rigor and international perspective to my consulting practice.

I received a scholarship for an exchange program at Nagoya University in Japan, and have lived and worked in Germany for 10 years, with additional experience in Poland. My diverse professional background includes logistics, data analytics, and renewable energy sectors, giving me firsthand understanding of the challenges and opportunities of building a life abroad.

My multilingual capabilities in English, German, Japanese, and Indonesian allow me to provide tailored support for clients targeting different countries and educational systems.
