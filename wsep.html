Wind & Solar Energy Forecast

This webpage provides a 3-day forecast of wind and solar energy potential for a given location. Enter a location (city name or coordinates) to retrieve hourly data, visualized in a chart and tables. The daily accumulated energy is also displayed in a bar chart below.

Initial Conditions: The forecast is based on current weather conditions and predictive models provided by Open-Meteo. It considers factors such as wind speed, direct and diffuse solar radiation.

Forecast: The forecast predicts the potential energy output (in W/m² for hourly data and Wh/m² for daily accumulated energy) assuming ideal energy conversion. Actual energy production will depend on the efficiency of the energy conversion systems (e.g., solar panels, wind turbines).
