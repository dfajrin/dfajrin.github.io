Wind & Solar Energy Forecast

This webpage provides a forecast of wind and solar energy potential for a given location. Enter a location (city name or coordinates) and select a forecast duration (3, 7, 14, or 16 days) to retrieve hourly data, visualized in a chart and tables. The daily accumulated energy is also displayed in a bar chart below.

You can also select a specific date to retrieve historical forecast data for that date and the selected duration. Please note that historical data availability may vary.

Data Sources: The forecast is based on weather conditions and predictive models provided by Open-Meteo. It considers factors such as wind speed, direct and diffuse solar radiation.

Forecast/Historical Data: The data predicts the potential energy output (in W/m² for hourly data and Wh/m² for daily accumulated energy) assuming ideal energy conversion. Actual energy production will depend on the efficiency of the energy conversion systems (e.g., solar panels, wind turbines). The standard forecast API provides data for up to 7 days. For longer durations or when a specific date is selected, the Historical Weather API is used.
